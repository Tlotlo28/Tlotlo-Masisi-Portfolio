Connekkt

Connekkt is a location-aware platform helping creatives discover each other on a map. Users sign up, pick their field (each gets its own colour and shape on the map), upload portrait photos with built-in cropping, and find nearby collaborators using a hold-to-scan radius button. Built solo with FastAPI + PostgreSQL on the backend, vanilla JavaScript + Leaflet on the frontend, JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and server-side Haversine distance queries. Deployed on Render and Cloudflare Pages. Zoom out to see other demo profiles. NB May take a few seconds to wake up since it's on a free Render plan, but it works!
